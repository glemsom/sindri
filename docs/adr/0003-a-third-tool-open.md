# 0003 — A third tool: `open` (superseded)

This record argued `open` as a third tool, for the reason ADR 0002 argues `skill` as a second
one: a permission the shell cannot carry. Its argument — the measured `xdg-open` that exits 0
inside the sandbox, the result word `started`, the refusals made in the call and not in the
shell, and the absence from a run with no window — is now §2 of
[`0002-two-tools.md`](0002-two-tools.md), where the tool set's decisions live. Read that.

Nothing is repeated here, on purpose: two places stating one fact are two places to disagree,
and a superseded record that keeps its copy is the drift that put the two records out of step
in the first place.
